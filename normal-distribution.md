# Normal distribution

[← Back to VaR](README.md) · [← Back to z-score](z-score.md)

It's the classic bell curve [[1]](#sources). Most outcomes sit close to the average, and extreme ones get rarer the further out you go.

It's symmetric around its mean [[1]](#sources), so being unlucky by a given amount is as likely as being lucky by the same amount.

It's fully described by two numbers, the **mean** and the **standard deviation** [[2]](#sources).

The "standard" normal is the special case where the mean is 0 and the standard deviation is 1 [[2]](#sources). That's the version z-tables are built on.
